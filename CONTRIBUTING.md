# Contributing

Thanks for wanting to make these skills better.

- **Found a bug?** [Open an issue](https://github.com/bisque-cloud/artifacts/issues)
  with what you asked your agent, what it ran, and what happened. Include your
  agent (Claude Code, Gemini CLI, Codex) and your OS. Never paste an Artifact
  link that is not public, a comment thread, or an API key into an issue —
  describe the shape of the problem instead.
- **Found a security problem?** Use
  [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/bisque-cloud/artifacts/security/advisories/new)
  rather than opening an issue. These skills decide who can open a page, so a
  report about them should not be public while it is unfixed. The advisory
  goes straight to the maintainers and stays private until there is a fix.
- **Have a fix or improvement?** Open a pull request — small and focused beats
  large and sweeping. For anything substantial, an issue first saves you from
  building something we can't take.

The rules at the end of each `SKILL.md` — confirming before anything that
removes access or content, never guessing an email address, updating the
existing Artifact instead of publishing a copy — are load-bearing rather than
stylistic. A change that removes one needs to say what replaces it.

We review every PR. Changes reach this repo through our release process, so
your PR may be closed with your change committed and credited rather than
merged in place — either way, you'll see your change (and your name on it) in
the repo and in the next release.
